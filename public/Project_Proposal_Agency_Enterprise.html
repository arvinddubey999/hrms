Smart Attendance & Task Management System

1. Executive Summary

This document details the functional scope, architecture, delivery timeline, and commercial estimation for building an enterprise-grade Smart Attendance & Task Management System. The system comprises a responsive Web Administration Portal built with Laravel 12 & MySQL, accompanied by a Flutter Cross-Platform Mobile App (Android & iOS).

2. Functional Modules

👥 Employee & Staff Management

  • Employee Directory with instant search and dynamic filters.
  • Auto-generated unique Employee Codes & Multi-Company Master.
  • Staff Profile with monthly attendance matrix calendar view.
  • Bulk Shift assignment & Bulk Attendance marking tools.

⏱️ Smart Attendance & Punch System

  • Single & Multiple Punch IN/OUT tracking per day.
  • GPS Location tracking (Geo-coordinates & Address).
  • Face Recognition Attendance integration.
  • Live Attendance Statistics Dashboard (Present, Absent, Late, Early, Leave).
  • Admin Manual Attendance Punch Override with remarks.

📋 Task Assignment & Workflow

  • Department-scoped Task Creation & Employee Assignment.
  • Priority levels (Low, Medium, High) & Due Date tracking.
  • Instant Email & Mobile Push Notifications to assigned staff.
  • Task detail timeline, status updates, and attachments.

🗺️ Live Tracking & Movement History

  • Real-time Employee GPS Live Location Tracking on Map.
  • Daily Movement Route Timeline with stop locations & distance.
  • Leave & WOP Request submission & Approval Workflow.
  • Master Shift configuration & Monthly Roster scheduling.

📊 Comprehensive Reports Engine

  • Daywise Attendance Report: Excel & PDF export with Company header branding.
  • Multiple Punch IN/OUT Report: Detailed timestamps breakdown per employee.
  • Monthly Summary ZIP Export: One-click bulk ZIP download of employee monthly cards.
  • Working Hours & Overtime Report: Cumulative monthly duration calculations.
  • Colorful Staff Monthly PDF: PDF download matching official header layouts.
